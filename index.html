01 — Now building · PlayPrismAI

Meet your players before you ship.

PlayPrismAI reads thousands of real Steam reviews of games like yours and turns them into evidence-grounded player personas, predicted reactions and a ranked map of risks and opportunities for your pitch — in minutes, not months.

$100M+to build a modern game
10 minfor a player to decide whether to refund
100M+Steam reviews already say what players want

No team can read them all. So studios rely on intuition and learn what players feel after launch. By then it's too late.

  1. Finds the eight closest shipped peers on Steam by genre, mechanics and theme.
  2. Harvests and embeds up to 1,000 real reviews per peer, then clusters them into cited player personas.
  3. Each persona “plays” the pitch: predicted hook, bounce and late-game engagement.
  4. An authenticity gate checks synthetic reactions against real launch data — if it fails, the pipeline stops.
  5. Ranks risks and opportunities two ways: what design should fix, and what marketing should brag about.

Every claim cites the real reviews it came from. Ten specialist agents, a resumable LangGraph pipeline and on-premises models, wrapped in a multi-user studio platform with drill-down chat and a one-page studio memo.

02 — Research programs

Three questions, one agenda.

Every paper, product and patent I have worked on answers one of three questions: how to make representations reliable under shift, how to adapt foundation models to video and structure efficiently, and how to deploy AI systems people can trust.

01 · Robust representations
Why do models that win benchmarks fail in the world — and how do we train representations that don't?

Robustness should be learned during pretraining, not patched afterwards. Adaptive adversaries (ASTrA, ICLR 2025), camera-free perspective robustness (Möbius-MPD, ECCV 2024) and label-efficient learning for medicine and mining.

+10%Robustness for SimCLR, DINO & CLIP · Möbius-MPD
+3–6%Adversarial robustness · ASTrA
20%Labels needed to match supervised · Magnification Prior
02 · Multimodal & video
How do we adapt large vision-language models to time, place and structure — without retraining them?

Most capability already sits inside frozen foundation models; the research problem is the small, well-placed adapter that unlocks it. DiA (NeurIPS 2026) for fine-grained video, VidTAG (CVPR 2026) for global-scale video geolocalization.

92.8%Top-1 on Diving48 with ~3.6M tunable params · DiA
2.17×Throughput vs. AIM · DiA
+20%Accuracy at 1 km vs. GeoCLIP · VidTAG
03 · Agentic & deployed AI
What does it take for AI to be trusted inside real products and decisions?

Reliability is a property of the system, not the model. Grounding, evaluation gates and the right human checkpoints decide whether AI can be deployed — from Samsung's recognition engine to PlayPrismAI's authenticity gate.
