    <div class="eyebrow">01 · Augmented reality</div>
    <div class="meta" style="margin-top: 16px"><strong>US 11,024,263 B2</strong>Granted 2021 · United States</div>
    <p class="links-row"><a class="link" href="https://patents.google.com/patent/US11024263B2" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google Patents →</a></p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h2>Method and apparatus for adjusting <em>augmented reality content</em>.</h2>
    <p>Monitors how a user views objects, estimates how long they will look at the next one, and sizes the AR content to fit that viewing window. Hidden Markov Models over eye and head movement predict attention in real time, so AR content stays personal and context-aware.</p>
    <div class="pf-cap" style="margin: 22px 0 8px">Cited by</div>

<section class="pf-chapter">

    <div class="eyebrow">03 · Virtual reality</div>
    <div class="meta" style="margin-top: 16px"><strong>US 10,706,632 B2</strong>Granted 2020 · United States</div>
    <p class="links-row"><a class="link" href="https://patents.google.com/patent/US10706632B2" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google Patents →</a></p>

  <div>
    <h2>Managing <em>wide-view content</em> in a virtual reality environment.</h2>
    <p>Shows the part of a 360° scene the user is looking at alongside the full wide view, rendered on a convex projection with in-focal and out-focal views, so context is never lost while moving between viewpoints.</p>
    <div class="pf-cap" style="margin: 22px 0 8px">Cited by</div>
